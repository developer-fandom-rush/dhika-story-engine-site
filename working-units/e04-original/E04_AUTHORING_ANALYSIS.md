## 1. What Episode 04 actually does

E04 is not one continuous erotic lane. It has a two-stage architecture:

1. **P1 finishes the immediate Chotu aftereffect from E03.**
2. **P2–P10 switches foreground to the twins and builds a separate physical-access grammar.**

The Chotu route is not resolved or erased when the twins take over. It remains paused at a stronger internal state.

## 3. Why the twins route is not a copy of Chotu

Chotu's E03 route is mainly:
trust/familiarity → caught gaze → teasing → private curiosity → mutual awareness → kiss/awkwardness → deliberate visual invitation.

Twins E04 route is different:
post-accident affection → relaxed homewear → heat/comfort logic → deliberate dupatta reduction → caught gaze → requested outfit → negotiated pallu removal → explicit body compliments → selective questions/refusals → secrecy → future request.

The twins route is more **negotiated, conversational and precedent-based**. It frequently uses:
- “you already saw…”
- “we trust you / do you trust us?”
- request → objection → smaller/larger request
- embarrassment mixed with joking
- confidentiality.

Do not write future twins scenes as if they are simply another Chotu-style “she wants him to look” route unless RAW actually moves there.
