Part 1 Review

Episode 02 opens directly from E01’s emotional endpoint. Dhika is still in the same “cloud9” state when the bell rings, but seeing John produces immediate guilt. She hides the feeling and resumes her familiar domestic role, sending her exhausted husband to bathe and checking whether he has eaten. John’s affectionate reunion with Pappu and his later sleep on Dhika’s shoulder restore the household’s warm surface. The important change is not a marital rupture: it is the new information gap inside an otherwise caring family scene.
