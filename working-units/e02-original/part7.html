Part 7 Review

When Uncle finds her outside, Dhika says she came to help with the milk and lets him believe she never entered. Back at lunch she feeds Pappu while replaying the scene. Her practical response is to leave milk with Aunty from now on. She goes home with John and deliberately does not disclose the incident to protect Uncle’s image.
