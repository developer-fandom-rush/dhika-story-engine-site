Writer Guidance

Use the exact Uncle progression: Part 1 concealed attraction + habitual visual ogling → Part 3 first documented covert personal-item behavior.

Keep mental and physical layers separate: fantasy is internal; handling/sniffing is the behavioral evidence.

Preserve asymmetric relationship state: Dhika-side trust remains unchanged until she receives evidence; Uncle-side hidden behavior has changed.

Preserve knowledge gap: reader/Uncle know; Dhika/Aunty/John do not as of L70.

Do not inflate labels: first ≠ major, first ≠ repeat, first ≠ normalized.
