## 1. Episode Structure / Narrative Architecture

E06 is built as a controlled route weave rather than a single uninterrupted lane:

1. **Expected Chotu route** — Dhika protects Saturday for painting and specifically looks forward to Chotu.
2. **Bobby solo route** — Maami delays Chotu, creating rare one-on-one Bobby access.
3. **Bobby + Chotu shared route** — Chotu arrives while Bobby is still there; painting merges the two established routes in one space.
4. **Chotu private route** — Bobby leaves; the Episode’s highest sexual-knowledge and physical threshold occurs one-on-one.
5. **Aftermath route** — Chotu exits unresolved; Dhika’s strong arousal is immediately counterweighted by guilt when John’s photo re-enters her awareness.

The transitions are driven by ordinary logistics—temple outing, store delay, Physics competition, heat, painting, paint spill, clothing cleanup—not by arbitrary scene switching.

## 2. Escalation & Pacing Design

- The Episode begins with anticipation rather than action. Delaying Chotu makes the reader hold the E05 handoff while Bobby develops independently.
